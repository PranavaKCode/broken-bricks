## A1 (2026-10-11, sensitivity added before any metric was computed; kind: implementation)

What was seen: only the first lines of `gene2pubtator3.gz` (five tab-separated columns `PMID, Type, ConceptID, Mentions, Resource`; no header; Resource = "PubTator3") and the `Homo_sapiens.gene_info.gz` and UniProt headers. No attention counts had been computed.
Issue: the file is keyed on PMID and does not say whether an annotation came from the abstract or from PMC full text.
Decision: the pre-registered literal definition D1 stays the primary attention metric. We add sensitivity **S8**: `A_g_abs` counts a (PMID, gene) pair only if at least one of its Mentions strings occurs (case-insensitive, whole-token) in the title or abstract of the corpus record. The report gives the fraction of pairs that pass, the Spearman correlation of `A_g` with `A_g_abs`, and the Jaccard overlap of the two top-25 under-explored lists, and states which headline claims hold under both. No result switches definition.
Thresholds and decision rules: unchanged.

## A2 (2026-10-12 00:19 EDT, deviation from D1; made before any attention-vs-genetics comparison, ranking, Gini, biodomain share or event-study number was computed)

What was seen (stage A output only): (i) `gene2pubtator3.gz` mixes annotations from PMC full text with abstract annotations: the largest PMID has 3,234 human genes, and a PMID with more than 40 genes has only 4.5% of its (PMID, gene) pairs verifiable in the title/abstract. (ii) The share of pairs whose mention string appears in the title/abstract is 85-90% for 1985-2000 papers, falls to 17-26% for 2010-2024 papers, and is 65% for 2025 papers (recent months not yet full-text annotated). So the literal D1 count is **not abstract-level**, and it is **biased in time** (it inflates 2005-2024 papers against older and newest ones). (iii) The Resource column also carries curated gene2pubmed/MeSH/BioGRID links with an empty Mentions field. (iv) Top genes under the literal count: APP 66,068, MAPT 44,132, APOE 27,237, PSEN1 11,876. Pilot-reproduction results (amyloid shares within 3 pp; TREM2 flag counts 0 in 2012 and 18 in 2013, which fails L-V2) were also seen. No genetic score had been joined to any attention count.
Why this is a deviation: D1 as written (all PMIDs for which the file lists the gene) does not deliver what 06 section 5 requires ("abstract-level, normalized gene IDs").
New primary attention metric (D1'): `A_g` counts a (PMID, human Gene ID) pair only if at least one of its Mentions strings is found in the title or abstract of the corpus record after normalisation (Unicode NFKC, lower case, Greek letters spelled out, hyphens/slashes/brackets/underscores read as spaces, collapsed whitespace, token boundaries), with a second pass that ignores spaces for mention strings of at least 5 characters. Curated pairs with an empty Mentions field are not abstract-level and are not counted. This supersedes A1's strict verifier (A1's strict count is kept as sensitivity S8a, the literal D1 count as S8b).
Recall check of the verifier: the verified share for 1985-2000 papers (where every annotation comes from the abstract) is reported next to the result; the V2 audit (50 pairs) now also samples 10 pairs that the verifier rejected, to estimate false rejections.
Thresholds, decision rules, the genetic metric, biodomain definitions, event study, expectations L1-L5, L-V1, L-V2 and all other sensitivities are unchanged. L-V2 (TREM2 reproduction) is reported as it comes out; a diagnostic comparison with direct PubMed field-tag counts is added as V1b and does not replace the check.

## A3 (2026-10-12, implementation fixes and clarifications made while producing the final tables; no threshold or decision rule changed)

All items below were made before the final result tables were written. The first stage-B run was overwritten by the corrected run; the only differences between them are described in item 1.

1. **Ensembl-to-NCBI Gene mapping bug (fixed).** The first stage-B run showed PLCG2 with genetic score 0 in the event-study table although Open Targets lists it at 0.909. Cause: 261 Ensembl IDs are shared by several NCBI Gene records (for example PLCG2 and the ncRNA LOC105369213; ANO5 and LOC102723370) and a plain dictionary kept the last record. Rule now used: prefer the record whose symbol equals the source symbol, then protein-coding, then the lowest Gene ID. Effect on results: the two LOC records disappeared from the under-explored list (they had been carrying PLCG2's and ANO5's scores), PLCG2 and ANO5 appear with their scores, the Spearman estimate moved from 0.208 to 0.207, the TREM2 DiD from 3.73 to 3.67. L1 to L5 outcomes are unchanged.
2. **Agora genes without a record return the JSON body `null` with HTTP 200** (not 404). The first fetcher treated that as a failure and retried each such gene about 2 minutes, which stalled it at about 5,000 of 5,634 genes. Fixed: `null` means "not in Agora", recorded as status 404. Final coverage of the primary universe (3,101 Ensembl IDs): 3,010 with a record, 91 not in Agora. Agora requests were made at 2.5 requests/s or less; 11 genes outside the primary universe stayed unfetched.
3. **Attention-metric A2** (see above) is the primary metric in every table. The literal D1 counts, A1's strict-verifier counts and the ortholog merge are reported as sensitivities S8b, S8a and S4.
4. **Leiden resolution (exploratory).** Resolution 1.0 gave 39 clusters (modularity 0.793). For a usable number of labelled regions we re-ran at resolutions 5, 15 and 40 (149, 355, 726 clusters) and kept 15 (target about 300). Cluster keyword labels use class-based TF-IDF with a minimum term count and a stop-word list (no LLM). Macro-topics for the stream graph are 8 k-means groups of the 355 cluster centroids (seed 20261011).
5. **Gini bootstrap bias.** The Poisson bootstrap over PMIDs is biased upward for the zero-heavy Gini (bootstrap mean 0.9584 vs estimate 0.9550), so the percentile interval does not contain the estimate. We report the bias and the basic (reflected) interval next to the percentile one (`concentration_gini.csv`). No decision depends on it.
6. **Exploratory table added after seeing the pre-registered biodomain result** (`exploratory_biodomain_strong_genes_G05.csv`): where the 173 genes with genetic score >= 0.5 sit across biodomains compared with the attention share. Labelled exploratory in the report; not part of any pre-registered claim.
7. **Corpus composition.** The corpus has 236,973 records (PubMed ESearch total on 2026-10-12: 237,061): 88 records flagged `hasabstract` had empty abstract text and were dropped; 414 records are NCBI Bookshelf documents (record_type = book) and were kept because the query returns them. Years run 1954 to 2027 (28 records carry a 2027 issue date); 2026 is partial (15,426 records).
8. **Where compute ran.** All downloads, parsing and statistics ran in Slurm jobs. Several short CPU jobs used the `mit_quicktest` partition (15 min limit) because `mit_normal` and `mit_preemptable` queues were long. A few light interactive checks (API probes with a handful of requests, reading the head of files, merging 60 audit judgments) ran on the login node.

1. **Analysed set (C1 applied).** `day0_coverage.csv` gives 81-84 donors with >= 50 nuclei in each of the 11 non-vascular subclasses (threshold in C1: >= 40). The H1 denominator is therefore **11** (Microglia-PVM, Astrocyte, Oligodendrocyte, OPC, Sst, Pvalb, Vip, Lamp5, L2/3 IT, L4 IT, L5 IT). Endothelial and VLMC are excluded a priori (C1).

Variants rebuild the kNN graph on the retained cells. H1 and PC1 are decided on the primary (all-donor) runs.
3. **CNA settings not fixed by the protocol.** `cna` 0.2.6, `cna.tl.association` with default NAM settings and default `ks` (1..4 NAM PCs for 84 donors), no batch argument, `Nnull = 10,000` permutations for the global P (the minimum attainable P is 1/10,001 = 9.999e-5; the package computes the local FDR from the first 1,000 permutations regardless), `seed = 0`, `sc.pp.neighbors(use_rep="X_scVI", n_neighbors=30, random_state=0)`. FDR flags: per-cell empirical FDR (`obs[cna_*_fdr]`) <= 0.05 and <= 0.10; cells outside the retained neighborhoods have no `ncorr` and are never flagged. Covariates: age, sex (male = 1), PMI, and method fraction = share of the donor's nuclei of that subclass with assay "10x multiome" (C2). 4. **Operational definitions for PC1 (fixed here, before the full concordance was computed).**

   b. "Sst neighborhoods with negative ncorr (depleted)": the mean `ncorr` over all Sst cells is < 0 **and**, among cells with FDR <= 10%, negative-`ncorr` cells outnumber positive ones.
   c. "Supertypes that SEA-AD calls credible" (C3): MTG rows of `pertpy_summary_CPS_Local.20260622.csv` whose `Local Model` value is finite and non-zero (SEA-AD's own summary table; NaN = not modelled, 0 = not credible). Check behind this choice: in the per-run results all 42 supertypes that are credible (Bayesian FDR 5% on the inclusion probabilities) in >= 50% of their reference-cell-type runs have a non-zero summary value, and the summary's sign agrees with the median effect over the runs for 96% of non-zero values.
   d. Sensitivity (reported next to the primary, never replacing it): credible = Bayesian-FDR-5% credible in >= 50% of the reference-cell-type runs of the per-run results (`*_Supertype_results.csv`), effect = median `Final Parameter` over runs. If primary and sensitivity disagree on pass/fail, both are reported prominently.
   e. "Microglia expansion" is reported as a descriptive PC1 supplement, not a pass criterion: the SEA-AD disease-associated supertype Micro-PVM_3-SEAAD has mean `ncorr` > 0 and more FDR<=10% positive than negative cells.
   Correction to the Day-0 report: it states SEA-AD calls no credible Micro-PVM supertype in MTG and that `Local Model` does not track the aggregated effects. The first statement used a per-run HDI heuristic; the summary table lists three Micro-PVM supertypes with non-zero values. The second came from counting summary zeros (not credible) as sign disagreements.

6. **Figures.** Red = positive `ncorr` (abundance rises with CPS), blue = negative, diverging with a neutral midpoint, symmetric limits +/- max|ncorr| of each panel, SEA-AD's native `X_umap` of the aged cells; panels that mask to FDR <= 10% show the other cells in neutral grey.

## A6 (2026-10-12 06:05 EDT, Day 2: donor brick profiles H2, H2b, H2c; kind: implementation clarifications written BEFORE any NAM-PC, brick score, residual, reliability or discordance number was computed)

What was known when this entry was written. All Day-1 results (PC1, H1, Milo, floor). The Day-0 report. Nothing else: no phenotype-free NAM, no ridge score, no residual, no donor-level co-pathology regression and no H2c comparison had been computed or looked at by the analysis agent. The preregistration (sections 1.1 to 1.3) fixes the hypotheses and thresholds; this entry only fixes the operational details that the protocol leaves open. No threshold, covariate set or exclusion is changed. Where the protocol gives a range (k = 10 to 20 PCs) the primary value is fixed here and the rest of the range is a sensitivity.

1. **Columns and donors.** B has one column per non-vascular subclass of H1 (11 columns; the protocol's "84 x 10" predates C1). A donor has a score in column j only if it has >= 50 nuclei of that subclass (brick-score rule; 81 to 84 donors per column). Primary variant `all`: every aged donor meeting the rule; kNN graph rebuilt on the retained nuclei (identical to Day-1 `all` for the four subclasses where all 84 donors qualify, identical to Day-1 `ge50` for the others). Variant `nosad`: additionally without the 11 Severely Affected Donors, graph rebuilt. Both variants run end to end. Verdicts for H2, H2b, H2c are taken on `all`; `nosad` is reported next to each; if the two disagree, both are stated prominently.
2. **Phenotype-free NAM.** kNN on `X_scVI`, k = 30, `random_state = 0` (as Day 1). NAM computed by the `cna` 0.2.6 routine `_nam` (self weight 1; number of diffusion steps from cna's own kurtosis stopping rule on the full retained data, then held fixed for the half-sample NAMs), with no phenotype, no covariate and no batch (`covs = None`). The columns are standardised and the 84 x 84 Gram matrix decomposed as in cna (`_resid_nam` with `covs = None`). PC scores = U * sqrt(singular value). Primary k = 15 PCs (midpoint of 10 to 20); sensitivities k = 10 and k = 20; sensitivity `covNAM` = NAM residualised for age, sex, PMI and method fraction (the Day-1 covariates) before the SVD, k = 15; sensitivity `foldPCA` = PCA refitted on the training donors of each fold with the held-out donors projected, k = 15. Literal reading of the protocol: the primary NAM is not covariate-adjusted, so that technical axes stay visible to the technical-covariate regression (item 9).
3. **Brick score.** Ridge regression of CPS_Local on the k PC scores (scores divided by one global constant, the SD of PC1, so that a single alpha grid applies), penalty chosen by inner leave-one-out cross-validation within the training donors (`RidgeCV`, alphas = logspace(-4, 4, 33)). 5-fold by donor; fold assignment from `numpy.random.default_rng(20261011).permutation` over the 84 donors in sorted-ID order, fold = position mod 5, the same assignment in every subclass and variant (donors absent from a column simply do not appear). B = the out-of-fold prediction. Stability of B to the fold assignment is reported using 20 further random assignments (mean pairwise Pearson r of B); B itself always comes from the seed-20261011 assignment.
4. **Reliability and filters.** C4 as written: 20 random splits of each donor's nuclei into halves (seeds 20261011 + s), B recomputed on each half with the same graph, the same number of diffusion steps and the same folds, Pearson r between the two half vectors, median over splits, uncorrected. Out-of-fold rho = Spearman(B, CPS_Local); its permutation P uses 1,000 permutations of CPS_Local over donors with the whole 5-fold ridge repeated (the NAM PCs do not depend on the phenotype). A subclass column is retained for the residual analyses iff split-half r >= 0.6 and rho > 0.4 and permutation P < 0.01. The H2 reliability criterion (">= 4 subclasses with split-half r >= 0.6") is judged on r alone, as preregistered; the rho/P filter only decides which columns enter the structure analyses.
5. **Residuals.** R_j = B_j - f_j(CPS_Local), f_j a local-quadratic LOESS of B_j on the true CPS_Local among the donors of column j (tricube weights, span 0.75, degree 2, Gaussian family, no interpolation, as R's `loess` defaults). Z_j = R_j / SD(R_j). Residual reliability rr_j = median over the 20 splits of Pearson r between R_j(half A) and R_j(half B) (R computed from each half's B with the same LOESS recipe).
6. **Parallel analysis.** Eigenvalues of the correlation matrix of Z over the retained columns (donors with a score in every retained column), compared rank by rank with the 95th percentile of the eigenvalues of 1,000 within-column permutations of Z (Horn). Number of factors = number of leading observed eigenvalues above that percentile. Criterion A: >= 2 factors.
7. **Discordance.** Glia composite = mean of Z over the retained glial columns (Microglia-PVM, Astrocyte, Oligodendrocyte, OPC); neuron composite = mean of Z over the retained neuronal columns (Sst, Pvalb, Vip, Lamp5, L2/3 IT, L4 IT, L5 IT) (C5); donors with a missing column use the mean of the available ones, and need at least one column in each class; each composite is re-standardised over donors (z_G, z_N). Discordant iff sign(z_G) != sign(z_N) and |z_G| > 1.5 and |z_N| > 1.5. Two nulls, both using the same retained columns: (i) permutation null, 2,000 within-column permutations of Z (the same device as the parallel analysis); (ii) reliability-matched null, 10,000 simulated datasets in which every donor has one true residual shared by all columns and each column adds independent Gaussian noise whose share of variance is 1 - rr_j (Z*_j = sqrt(rr_j) F + sqrt(1 - rr_j) e_j, rr_j floored at 0): the discordance expected if the apparent glia-versus-neuron differences were measurement noise alone. Expected rate = mean over simulations; P = (1 + number of simulated rates >= observed) / (1 + number of simulations). Criterion B: observed discordance >= 10% of donors and the reliability-matched expectation <= 5%. **H2 passes iff (>= 4 subclasses with split-half r >= 0.6) AND (criterion A OR criterion B)**, on `all`.
8. **Severely Affected Donor recovery.** Robust Mahalanobis distance (`MinCovDet`, support fraction 0.75, `random_state = 0`) over the donors x retained-column matrix of B (z-scored; missing scores replaced by the column median, because a missing score means < 50 nuclei, which is itself the SAD signature), and over Z. Recovery is reported as AUC of the distance for the 11 flagged donors against the rest, precision among the 11 highest distances, and a label-permutation P (10,000). Declared pass for the check: AUC >= 0.75 on B. This is a validity check, not a hypothesis.
9. **Technical covariates.** Per donor: total final nuclei, RIN, PMI, NeuN+ fraction, multiome fraction (method), and sequencing saturation (nuclei-weighted donor mean of the library-level `GEX_sequencing_saturation`; libraries with no value are ignored; a donor with none gets the median and is flagged). Each residual axis (every column Z_j, z_G, z_N, the discordance index delta = z_G - z_N, and the parallel-analysis factor scores) is regressed on the six covariates (standardised; nuclei count log10). An axis "tracks technical covariates" and is dropped from the claims if the joint F-test has P < 0.05 or any single covariate has Spearman P < 0.05/6.
10. **H2b.** Outcome: delta = z_G - z_N (signed; |delta| as secondary). Predictors, entered jointly in one OLS model with CPS_Local as an extra covariate: LATE stage (0 to 3; "unclassifiable" missing), Lewy body disease (0 none / not identified, 1 amygdala-predominant or brainstem-predominant, 2 limbic, 3 neocortical), arteriolosclerosis (0 to 3), microinfarcts (log1p of the total microinfarct count outside gross), CAA (0 to 3), and the Abeta-minus-pTau difference of CPS_Local components (z-scored). Verdict: H2b passes iff the omnibus F-test of the six predictors (nested against CPS_Local alone) has P < 0.05 or the Abeta-minus-pTau coefficient has P < 0.05; per-predictor P are Holm-adjusted over the six; adjusted R^2 gain reported. Secondary: the same for z_G, z_N and each retained Z column; APOE4, cognitive status (dementia), last CASI and MMSE regressed with CPS_Local as covariate.
11. **H2c.** High-ADNC donors (42): resilient = "No dementia" (11), demented = "Dementia" (31). Outcome: neuronal brick residual = mean Z over the retained neuronal columns (C5). Effect: Hedges' g for (resilient minus demented), so g < 0 means healthier; 95% CI from the Hedges-Olkin standard error and, alongside, a percentile bootstrap (10,000); one-sided Welch P (resilient < demented) and one-sided permutation P (10,000). Sensitivities: all neuronal columns regardless of the filter; each subclass; glia composite; adjusting for CPS_Local; `nosad` (the SAD donors are all High ADNC).
12. **Mantis donors floor.** 84 rows. Coordinates = first two principal components of the matrix of Z (retained columns, missing = 0), with a UMAP of the same matrix (n_neighbors 10, min_dist 0.3, seed 20261011) saved in extra columns. `vector_columns` = the 11 B columns z-scored, missing = column median. Labels: discordance label, per-subclass B, R, Z, co-pathologies, cognition, APOE, SAD and resilient flags.
13. **Where compute runs.** Graph, NAM, half-sample NAMs: Slurm jobs on ORCD (script `bricks/day2_nam.py`). Ridge, filters, residuals, nulls, regressions, figures: local, from the saved PC-score arrays (`bricks/day2_bricks.py`).

## A7 (2026-10-12 06:10 EDT, Day 3: programs, MAGMA (H3) and APOE4 cholesterol (H4); kind: implementation clarifications written BEFORE any program gene, MAGMA gene or set result, specificity covariate or cholesterol score was computed)

The P/N file built on Day 0 is used as is (N = n_cas + n_con, the single P = 0 SNP dropped). Sensitivity: N_eff = 4 / (1/n_cas + 1/n_con) per SNP rebuilt from the harmonised file.
4. **Cell-type specificity covariates.** From the 13 SEA-AD pseudobulk objects, MTG rows of aged donors, raw counts summed over rows and donors per cell type (Immune = Microglia-PVM, Astrocyte, Oligodendrocyte, OPC, Sst, Pvalb, Vip, Lamp5, L2/3 IT, L4 IT, L5 IT, Endothelial, VLMC-Perivascular), CPM per cell type; genes with total CPM >= 1 over the 13 types; specificity = CPM_t / sum over types; the MAGMA covariate = rank-based inverse normal transform of the specificity across genes, one column per cell type. Positive control: gene-property (`--gene-covar`) test of each cell type, one-sided positive (MAGMA default). **H3 positive control passes iff the Microglia covariate has P < 1e-4.** Gene size, density and sample-size covariates are MAGMA's own defaults.
5. **Program tests.** MAGMA competitive set analysis (`--set-annot` + `--gene-covar`), one-sided positive: marginal, and conditional on the specificity covariate of the program's own subclass (`condition=<subclass>`). Open question (microglia red program conditional on Microglia specificity): reported as enriched beyond specificity iff the one-sided conditional P < 0.05. Neuronal red and blue programs (14 sets; the prediction is "damage without genetic fingerprints"): the prediction is declared to hold iff no neuronal program has a Holm-adjusted (over those 14) conditional P < 0.05; any nominal P < 0.05 is listed. Glial programs other than microglia red are descriptive.
6. **APOE sensitivity.** Genes with any part in chr19:44,000,000-46,000,000 (build 37) removed from the gene-level results before the gene-property and set analyses (positive control and programs rerun).
7. **Open Targets cross-check.** Genes with Open Targets 26.09 `genetic_association` score >= 0.5 for MONDO_0004975 (from the literature study's `results/gene_master.csv`). For each program: overlap count with that set among program genes in the subclass's expressed universe; null = 10,000 draws of a gene set of the same size matched on mean log-normalised expression (20 quantile bins of the subclass's expressed genes, sampled without replacement within each bin, program genes excluded), one-sided empirical P = (1 + n_null >= obs) / 10,001, fold enrichment; repeated without the APOE-region genes.
8. **H4.** Oligodendrocyte pseudobulk, MTG rows, raw counts summed per donor over all rows (all 84 donors in the primary analysis, as in the protocol's 25 vs 59; sensitivities: donors with >= 50 oligodendrocyte nuclei, and without the 11 SAD). Module = the 20 genes listed in the preregistration. Donor CPM = module counts / total counts of the donor's pseudobulk x 1e6; module score = mean over the 20 genes of the z-scored (over donors) log2(CPM + 1). OLS: score ~ APOE4 carrier (genotype contains an epsilon 4) + CPS_Local + age + sex + PMI; one-sided test for the APOE4 coefficient > 0 (t distribution), two-sided 95% CI, standardised effect (coefficient / residual SD), and a Freedman-Lane permutation P (10,000). pydeseq2 (0.5.4): raw counts of the 20 module genes for the 84 donors, size factors from all genes (median of ratios on the full gene set, then passed on), design ~ APOE4 + CPS_Local + age + sex + PMI (continuous covariates z-scored), dispersion trend "mean" (20 genes are too few for the parametric trend), Wald test of APOE4 per gene, one-sided P for log2FC > 0; plus the same model on the summed module count (one feature) as the module-level effect with its 95% CI. Reported as a replication attempt whatever the outcome (preregistration).
9. **Figures and exports.** `fig2_brick_matrix`, `fig3_discordant_pairs` (two donors matched on CPS_Local with opposite glia/neuron residuals, chosen by an automatic rule written in the code before it is run: smallest |CPS difference| among pairs with opposite discordance sign, ties by the larger |z_G - z_N| sum), `fig4_genetic_fingerprints`, `fig5_apoe4_oligo_cholesterol`, light and dark; `donor_bricks.csv`, `programs.gmt`, `claims_sheet.csv`, the donors floor CSV.
